International Payments

How international transfers and SWIFT work

SWIFT is a secure financial messaging network banks use to exchange payment instructions. It is not itself a bank account or a guarantee that a transfer will arrive instantly.

Sending an international bank transfer

  1. Confirm that your account supports international outgoing transfers and the destination country and currency.
  2. Enter the recipient's legal name, bank name, account number or IBAN where applicable, and the recipient bank's SWIFT/BIC code.
  3. Add any required bank address, intermediary-bank details or payment reference supplied by the recipient.
  4. Check the exchange rate, transfer fee, any intermediary-bank charges and the estimated arrival window before you authorise.
  5. Save the payment confirmation or reference so you can follow up if the receiving bank needs more information.

What to know before you send

  • Recipient details must match the receiving bank's records. Incorrect details can delay or return a payment.
  • Currency conversion may affect the amount received. The recipient or intermediary banks may deduct additional fees.
  • Processing can take several business days and may be affected by weekends, holidays, compliance checks and the banks involved.
  • Never share passwords, one-time codes or full card details with someone claiming to help process a transfer.

Only advertise SWIFT transfers, account features, rates or timelines that your institution is authorised and operationally able to provide. Confirm current terms with the bank before sending money.

Application process

Signing in lets an applicant access their account. It does not guarantee approval or immediate disbursement.

1

Create an account or sign in

Use the secure customer portal. Never share your password or one-time verification code with anyone.

2

Choose a programme

Provide the requested loan amount, purpose, income, expenses, and supporting documents through the authenticated application flow.

3

Assessment and offer

The lender verifies information and assesses affordability. If eligible, review the APR/interest rate, all fees, total repayment, instalments, and due dates.

4

Accept and receive funds

Funds are disbursed only after required checks and acceptance of the formal agreement. Timing depends on verification and payment processing.

Eligibility and documents

Requirements depend on the loan programme, applicant location, and applicable lending rules. Applicants may be asked for:

  • ✓ Government-issued photo identification
  • ✓ Proof of current residential address
  • ✓ Recent payslips, bank statements, or business financial records
  • ✓ Details of monthly expenses, existing debts, and repayment source
  • ✓ Additional documents relevant to the selected loan purpose

Before accepting a loan

Read the written agreement and compare the annual percentage rate (APR) where applicable, interest method, origination and late fees, total amount repayable, instalment dates, early-repayment conditions, and consequences of missed payments.

Borrow only an amount you can reasonably repay. Approval, interest rates, and disbursement times are not guaranteed.

Is the loan approved instantly? +

No approval or funding time is guaranteed. Even when an application is started online, identity checks, affordability assessment, document review, and payment processing may be required.

Can I apply without an account? +

You need to sign in to the customer portal to begin an application. If you are new, use Create an account. Your website's registration and authentication routes must be configured for these links to work.

Are interest rates and fees fixed on this page? +

No. This page does not invent or promise rates, fees, loan limits, or terms. The lender must provide the actual pricing and repayment schedule in a formal offer before you accept.

What happens if I am not eligible? +

The lender should communicate the decision and any available next steps in line with applicable rules. Do not submit false information or pay an unofficial fee to change a decision.
