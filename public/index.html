FAQ

Common questions

What do I need to open an international account? +

Valid government-issued ID, proof of address, and a completed application. Business accounts also require incorporation documents.

Which currencies can I hold? +

The International plan supports up to 12 major currencies within a single account, with live conversion rates shown before you transact.

How long does account approval take? +

Most applications are reviewed within 1–2 business days once all documents are submitted.

Is online banking available outside the Bahamas? +

Yes — available globally, 24/7, secured with multi-factor authentication.

Are there fees for international transfers? +

Transfer fees vary by currency and destination and are shown upfront before you confirm any transaction.

Can non-residents of the Bahamas open an account? +

Yes. West Pac Global specializes in serving clients who live, work, or do business outside the Bahamas, subject to standard identity verification.

What is the minimum deposit to open an account? +

Minimum opening deposits start at $500 for the Multi-Currency Account; see the Accounts page for details by account type.

How do I contact my relationship manager? +

International and Business account holders are given direct phone and email contact details for their named relationship manager after account approval.
