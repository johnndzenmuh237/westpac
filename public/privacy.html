1. What we collect

To open and run your account we record your legal name, date of birth, nationality, residential address, contact details, identification details, and information about your occupation, source of funds and expected activity.

2. How we use it

We use this information to open and manage your account, verify your identity, meet legal and regulatory obligations, prevent fraud, and provide customer support.

3. Who can see it

Your information is accessible only to authorised West Pec Global staff who need it to run your account. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Keeping it safe

Passwords are stored in hashed form and sign-in sessions expire automatically. West Pec Global will never ask you for your password by email, phone or message.

5. Your choices

You can keep your contact number up to date from your account page. To correct any other detail, contact support.

6. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the Support page after you sign in.
